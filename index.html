AI Safety Playbook for Small Businesses

Use AI safely in your small business — free, plain-English, ready to copy

By Emmanuel Diserbeau · Version 1.0, October 2026

Take the 2-minute risk check Get the policy template Download PDF (238 KB) Download Word (52 KB)

Not legal advice. This playbook is general guidance for small businesses. It is not legal, security, or compliance advice. If you handle health, financial, children’s, or government data, or work in a regulated industry, talk to a qualified lawyer or advisor before you finalize your policy.

What’s inside

Seven short sections for owners and managers of businesses with 5 to 200 people who don’t have a legal or security team. Start reading.

  1. 1

    Why AI Safety Matters

    The five risks that matter most: data leaks, IP, hallucinations, bias, and customer trust.

  2. 2

    AI Acceptable Use Policy (Template)

    A copy-ready AI Acceptable Use Policy with data classes and a “Never paste” list.

  3. 3

    AI Risk Self-Assessment

    Ten quick questions, scored 0–20, with an action plan for your risk level.

  4. 4

    High-ROI, Low-Risk Use Cases by Department

    SAFE, CAUTION, and AVOID use cases with example prompts for six departments.

  5. 5

    30-Day Safe Rollout Plan

    A week-by-week checklist to roll out AI safely in 30 days.

  6. 6

    Employee Quick-Reference Card

    A one-page do’s and don’ts card to print and hand to every employee.

  7. 7

    References and Further Reading

    Free frameworks and guidance from NIST, OWASP, CISA, the FTC, and others.

How to use it

  • 5 minutesRead Section 1 to understand the five risks.
  • 2 minutesScore yourself with the risk check.
  • 1 to 2 hoursFill in your AI Acceptable Use Policy.
  • 30 daysFollow the rollout plan and hand out the quick-reference card.
