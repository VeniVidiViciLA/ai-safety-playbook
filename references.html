7. References and Further Reading

These free, well-known resources go deeper. All links were checked in October 2026.

Risk management frameworks

  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0). U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology, January 2023. A voluntary framework built around four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
  • NIST AI 600-1: Generative AI Profile. NIST, July 2024. A companion to the AI RMF focused on risks of generative AI tools. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1
  • NIST AI RMF Playbook and AI Resource Center. Suggested actions for putting the framework into practice. https://airc.nist.gov/
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023, AI management systems. The international standard for managing AI in an organization (paid standard). https://www.iso.org/standard/42001
  • OECD AI Principles. The first intergovernmental standard on trustworthy AI, adopted 2019 and updated 2024. https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/ai-principles.html

Security guidance

  • OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications. Community list of top AI security risks, such as prompt injection and sensitive information disclosure. https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/
  • Deploying AI Systems Securely. Joint guidance from NSA, CISA, FBI, and international partners, April 2024. https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2024/04/15/joint-guidance-deploying-ai-systems-securely
  • AI Data Security: Best Practices for Securing Data Used to Train and Operate AI Systems. CISA and partners, May 2025. https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/ai-data-security-best-practices-securing-data-used-train-operate-ai-systems
  • Guidelines for Secure AI System Development. UK NCSC, CISA, and partners, 2023. https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/files/Guidelines-for-secure-AI-system-development.pdf

Consumer protection, copyright, and law

  • U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC): Artificial Intelligence. News, enforcement actions, and policy statements on AI, including cases about deceptive AI claims and fake reviews. https://www.ftc.gov/industry/technology/artificial-intelligence
  • U.S. Copyright Office: Copyright and Artificial Intelligence. Reports on AI and copyright, including Part 2 on copyrightability (January 2025). https://www.copyright.gov/ai/
  • EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Relevant if you sell to or operate in the EU. https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/dc8116a1-3fe6-11ef-865a-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
