From Section 3 of the playbook

AI Risk Self-Assessment

Answer each question honestly. Score 0 for Yes, 1 for Partly or Not sure, and 2 for No. Add up your score.

Private by design. Your answers stay in this browser tab. Nothing is sent anywhere, saved, or tracked. Closing the page erases them.

1Question 1: Do you have a written AI use policy that every employee has read and signed?
2Question 2: Do you know which AI tools your staff actually use today, including on phones?
3Question 3: Do staff use business accounts (not personal or free ones) for company work?
4Question 4: Have you turned off training on your data and set data retention, where vendors allow?
5Question 5: Do employees know what data must never be pasted into AI tools?
6Question 6: Is AI output checked by a person before it reaches customers or affects money, contracts, or safety?
7Question 7: Is AI kept out of final decisions about hiring, pay, discipline, or credit?
8Question 8: Is there one named person who owns AI decisions and approves new tools?
9Question 9: Do employees know how to report an AI mistake or data slip, without fear of blame?
10Question 10: Are MFA and an offboarding step (remove access when people leave) in place for AI accounts?

How scoring works
ScoreRisk levelWhat it meansWhat to do
0–5LOWYou have the basics covered.Keep it up. Review the policy every 6 months. Expand to more use cases from Section 4. Re-score each quarter.
6–12MEDIUMSome gaps that could cause a leak or a costly mistake.Fix every question you scored 2 within 30 days. Start with questions 3, 5, and 6. Follow the 30-day plan in Section 5.
13–20HIGHStaff are likely using AI with no guardrails.Act this week. Name an AI owner. Send a short interim "never paste" notice to all staff today. Buy business accounts for the tools people already use. Then follow Section 5.
