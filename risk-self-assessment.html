3. AI Risk Self-Assessment

Prefer to click? Take the interactive version: live score, your action plan, and a printable result. Runs entirely in your browser.

Take the 2-minute risk check

Answer each question honestly. Score 0 for Yes, 1 for Partly or Not sure, and 2 for No. Add up your score.

# Question Yes (0) Partly (1) No (2)
1 Do you have a written AI use policy that every employee has read and signed? ☐ ☐ ☐
2 Do you know which AI tools your staff actually use today, including on phones? ☐ ☐ ☐
3 Do staff use business accounts (not personal or free ones) for company work? ☐ ☐ ☐
4 Have you turned off training on your data and set data retention, where vendors allow? ☐ ☐ ☐
5 Do employees know what data must never be pasted into AI tools? ☐ ☐ ☐
6 Is AI output checked by a person before it reaches customers or affects money, contracts, or safety? ☐ ☐ ☐
7 Is AI kept out of final decisions about hiring, pay, discipline, or credit? ☐ ☐ ☐
8 Is there one named person who owns AI decisions and approves new tools? ☐ ☐ ☐
9 Do employees know how to report an AI mistake or data slip, without fear of blame? ☐ ☐ ☐
10 Are MFA and an offboarding step (remove access when people leave) in place for AI accounts? ☐ ☐ ☐

Your total score: ______ / 20

Score Risk level What it means What to do
0–5 LOW You have the basics covered. Keep it up. Review the policy every 6 months. Expand to more use cases from Section 4. Re-score each quarter.
6–12 MEDIUM Some gaps that could cause a leak or a costly mistake. Fix every question you scored 2 within 30 days. Start with questions 3, 5, and 6. Follow the 30-day plan in Section 5.
13–20 HIGH Staff are likely using AI with no guardrails. Act this week. Name an AI owner. Send a short interim "never paste" notice to all staff today. Buy business accounts for the tools people already use. Then follow Section 5.
